# Product memo: where to act first on merchant growth

Olist marketplace, orders placed Jan 2017 to Aug 2018. Sales in Brazilian reais (R$), item price only.

## Recommendation

1. **Fix the delivery promise before anything else.** Late delivery is the main experience problem, and it comes from peak months, not from a few bad sellers.
2. **Protect new sellers' first month with cheap, automated help.** Getting one-order starters to a second and third sale, and catching a bad first review early, is the clearest retention lever.
3. **Do not staff a large onboarding program.** The prize is about R$63,000 a year in the base case, roughly 1% of sales.

## Why

**Late delivery hurts reviews, and it is a timing problem.** 6.79% of delivered orders arrived after the promised date (6,532 / 96,204). Late orders averaged 2.27 stars against 4.29 on time, and each extra day late cost more. Lateness reached 19.0% in March 2018, when the promised delivery time was the shortest of the period (22.7 days) but actual delivery the slowest (16.2 days). The 37 sellers with 15%+ late orders carry only 9.4% of late orders, so policing sellers would miss most of the problem.

**New sellers decide early.** 75.9% of new sellers sell again within 3 months (1,832 / 2,414). Return is 66.5% after a one-order first month, 87.4% after 2 to 4 orders and 98.0% after 5+. Among sellers of the same first-month size, a bad first review goes with a far lower return (46.9% vs 90.1% for 2 to 4 order starters); a late first delivery barely moves it.

**The value sits with long-lived sellers.** Sellers active 7+ months carry 80.4% of sales.

## Sizing

One-order starters per year (983) × share moved up × sales gap (R$1,272 in average 6-month sales) × share of the gap credited as real.

| Case | Moved | Credited | Per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 5% | 25% | R$15,600 |
| Base | 10% | 50% | R$62,500 |
| High | 20% | 75% | R$187,600 |

Using medians instead of averages, the base case falls to about R$34,000.

## How to prove it

Offer the first-month help to a random half of new sellers and compare second-month activity and 6-month sales with the other half. That replaces the assumed "share credited" with a measured one.

## Limits

This is historical marketplace data, not software usage data: it shows order, delivery, review and seller behaviour, not feature adoption or website conversion. The seller comparisons are associations. Review score and lateness belong to the whole order, so on the 1,278 multi-seller orders each seller carries them.
